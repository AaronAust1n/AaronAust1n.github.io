## What the Conversation Covers

- California: taxes, governance, why nobody leaves, and the state as a "Saudi Arabia with tech instead of oil"
- America's "absolute stagnation"—the deficit, Social Security, and why every other country looking worse distracts from the real problems
- Why the tech industry stayed concentrated in California, and why AI is even more centralizing
- Passing the Turing test, and why almost nobody reacted like it mattered
- Bitcoin, great inventions, and the strange perception of change
- Energy density: nuclear power, fracking, and why dual-use risk killed reactor innovation
- The pyramids, ancient civilizations, ritual sacrifice, and the origins of kingship (Girard and Frazer)
- LSD, MKUltra, Ken Kesey, Timothy Leary, and how the 1960s counterculture began inside a CIA program
- Epstein, kompromat, and how secret clubs actually work
- Bill Gates, philanthropy as image-laundering, and the Nobel Prize
- The JFK assassination, Oswald, the "magic bullet," and the Warren Commission
- Fertility collapse—Korea at 0.7, demographic inversion, and why it may not reverse
- UFOs/UAPs: drones, antigravity, and what a mature civilization would actually do
- Artificial life as the likely end state of biological humanity
