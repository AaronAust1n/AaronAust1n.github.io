# The Peter Thiel Quote Corpus: Competition Is for Losers

> Compiled from public interviews, talks, and books. English wordings follow the original public statements where documented; the rest are faithful renderings.

*Published on April 7, 2026*

---

## Business Philosophy![Peter Thiel, photographed by Gage Skidmore](/images/posts/thiel-quotes/thiel-portrait.jpg)
*Image: Peter Thiel. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peter_Thiel_by_Gage_Skidmore.jpg) / Gage Skidmore, CC BY-SA 3.0.*



- "Competition is for losers."
- "All happy companies are different: each one earns a monopoly by solving a unique problem. All failed companies are the same: they failed to escape competition."
- "You are not a lottery ticket."
- "What important truth do very few people agree with you on?"
- "We wanted flying cars, instead we got 140 characters."
- "Every moment in business happens only once. The next Bill Gates will not build an operating system. The next Larry Page won't make a search engine."
- "Brilliant thinking is rare, but courage is in even shorter supply than genius."
- "A great company is a conspiracy to change the world."
- "Monopoly is the condition of every successful business."

## On Education

- "The university system in 2014 looks like the Catholic Church in 1514... a class of priests who don't do much work; people buy a kind of 'secular salvation' called a diploma by taking on enormous debt."

## Technology and Politics

- "If cryptocurrency is libertarian, then artificial intelligence is communist."
- "I no longer believe that freedom and democracy are compatible."

## Mimetic Theory, Applied

- "Humans are, at bottom, gigantic imitation machines."
- "The most ferocious conflicts arise from sameness, not difference."
- "When you stop looking up, you start looking around. And if you look around too much, that's no longer the wisdom of crowds—that's the madness of crowds."

## Technological Stagnation

- "Since the 1973 oil crisis, real wages in the West have been stagnant."
- "1969: Man walks on the moon; three weeks later, Woodstock. Scientific and technological progress stopped there, and the hippies took over the country."
- "If you believe that saying an idea out loud will get you silenced, that idea is probably right." (paraphrasing the Laughlin story)

## Eschatology

- "The Antichrist is not a person but a system—one that builds a single global government by stoking fear of the apocalypse."
- "Those who try to stop all scientific experiments and technological innovation in the name of safety are the true Luddites."
