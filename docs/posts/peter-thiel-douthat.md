## What the Conversation Covers

- The stagnation thesis, thirteen years after "The End of the Future"
- Why a society without growth "comes apart"—and what the middle class really is
- Risk, biotech, Alzheimer's research, and the case for letting the terminally ill take more risk
- Paying attention to politics as a form of "political venture capital"
- Elon Musk, Mars, and why 2024 ended the idea of Mars as an escape
- AI as the biggest exception to stagnation—and doubts about "superintelligence cascades"
- Why IQ may not be the binding constraint, and the risk of a "stagnant" AI
- Transhumanism: changing the body but not the soul—and why Thiel says its ambition is too small
- The Antichrist as a system, not a person: global governance, existential risk, and "peace and safety"
- Palantir, surveillance, and whether he is building the tools he fears
- God, history, and the space left for human freedom
